Permission-first real proof

Live evidence must earn its way into the decision.

A public phone number is not treated as permission. A live proof record is accepted only after explicit opt-in, CALL-E recipient binding, confidence/completion gates and transcript grounding.

01 · Before the call

PERMISSION
A private receipt must match the experiment and reviewed recipient, record ai_interview_opt_in=true, use an approved non-call channel, and include a timezone-aware consent time.

02 · After CALL-E

BIND + GROUND
Call ID, protocol hash and exact reviewed recipient must match. Answered evidence only counts when its structured result is backed by recipient-side transcript evidence.

03 · Public proof

REDACT + SEAL
Phone number, raw transcript, private consent statement and live quote text are withheld by default. The exported packet carries a deterministic SHA-256 content seal.

Verification boundary

A valid seal proves that the packet matches its recorded digest and that the public evidence satisfies the shipped integrity checks. It does not authenticate the operator, prove a trusted timestamp, independently attest consent, or make storage tamper-proof.

Claim boundary

CounterSignal demonstrates a pre-registered operational decision rule, not population inference or product-market fit. The benchmark compares deterministic decision policies, not commercial AI research products. Real-world business impact still requires larger empirical validation.
